About HitBeatRun

HitBeatRun (hitbeatrun.com) is run by Mind by Gab. HitBeatRun provides pre-recorded running videos with on-screen speed cues timed to the music. The videos are a guide: you choose and control your own low, medium and max speeds on your treadmill or outdoors.

Membership and billing

Members-only videos are offered through a paid membership (currently $4.99 per month) that is sold, billed and delivered through Fourthwall. By subscribing you also agree to Fourthwall's terms. Your membership renews automatically each month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time from your Fourthwall account; you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds

Because members get immediate access to digital content, payments are generally non-refundable. If you were charged in error or have a billing problem, contact us within 14 days and we will make it right.

Your health and safety

Running and interval training are physically demanding. Consult a doctor before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have a medical condition. Only run at speeds that are safe for you, use treadmill safety features, and stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. HitBeatRun is not medical advice, and you exercise at your own risk.

Membership is for one person's personal, non-commercial use. HitBeatRun videos, speed cues, course material, research and branding belong to Mind by Gab. Please don't download, copy, share, resell or publicly show members-only videos, or share your login. We may suspend access for accounts that break these terms.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

On this website we only collect what you choose to give us: your email address if you sign up for the newsletter, and anything you send us by email. Membership sign-ups, logins and payments happen on Fourthwall, which collects the details needed to run your account and process payments under its own privacy policy. We never see your full card details.

How we use it

We use your email to send the newsletter you asked for and to reply to your messages. Fourthwall shares with us the basic member information needed to provide your membership (such as your name, email and membership status).

What we never do

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Your choices
