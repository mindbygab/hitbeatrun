What is HitBeatRun?

Hit BeatRun is an innovative running algorithm designed to help you harness the power of rhythm to enhance your running experience.

By syncing your steps to carefully curated beats, it creates a seamless connection between music and movement, improving your pace, endurance, and overall performance.

Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned runner, Hit BeatRun transforms every run into an energizing and immersive journey.

Real-Time Performance Evaluation with an Athlete

We tested HitBeatRun with an indoor cycling instructor from Mixed Cycling Arts, evaluating its real-time performance. The algorithm seamlessly adjusted workout speed based on music energy, resulting in optimized performance and a more immersive training experience. This confirms the power of AI in enhancing fitness.

AI & Computer Vision in Action

By processing audio data through motion tracking, feature extraction, and image segmentation, the algorithm detects energy shifts in songs and translates them into real-time speed adjustments. This innovation ensures a fully immersive and efficient HIIT experience, seamlessly syncing your movement with the beat.
